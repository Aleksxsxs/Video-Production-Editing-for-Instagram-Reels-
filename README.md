
### 🎬 Educational Instagram Reel: Biology & Science Pop
A captivating, high-energy short-form vertical video created for Instagram Reels/TikTok focusing on biology and popular science. 

* **My Role:** Scripting, Self-Shooting, Editing, and Post-Production.
* **Software Used:** Adobe Premiere Pro.
* **Key Visual & Audio Techniques:**
  * Clean macro/closeup footages with professional lighting.
  * Dynamic jump-cuts and smooth transitions to keep the viewer’s retention high.
  * Engaging animated captions and kinetic typography for key terms.
  * Sound design (SFX) matched with upbeat background music.
  * Color grading to deliver a cinematic, high-quality look.
